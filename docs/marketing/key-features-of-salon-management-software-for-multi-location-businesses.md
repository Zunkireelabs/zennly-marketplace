<h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold">One calendar per location, live availability everywhere</h2>

<p class="text-muted-foreground">Multi-location scheduling falls apart when each branch keeps its own private diary. The core requirement is a calendar your team lists once and clients can read in real time — on Zennly, studios list one calendar and clients see availability and confirm in seconds, with no phone tag or message threads to reconcile. For a two- or five-site salon, that means front desks are no longer translating between systems, and a client searching for a treatment near them sees the branch that can actually take them today rather than the one closest to your head office.</p>

<h2 class="text-2xl font-semibold">Automated reminders to protect every branch's calendar</h2>

<p class="text-muted-foreground">No-shows compound across locations: an empty chair in one branch is invisible to the others. Automated reminders are the single highest-return feature for multi-site operators. One provider, North Fork Sauna Co., puts it plainly — "the automated reminders alone paid for themselves" — and reports cutting no-shows almost in half, with clients confirming through the app instead of quietly forgetting. Another owner describes the knock-on effect: between new client volume and fewer no-shows, the calendar "stopped feeling chaotic." For multi-location salons, that stability is what makes staffing and rota planning predictable.</p>
