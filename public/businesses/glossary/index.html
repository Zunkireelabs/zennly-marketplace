Glossary

Booking & operations glossary.

Plain-language definitions of the terms spas, salons, gyms and clinics use when choosing booking software, including the names used in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia, the UAE and Nepal.

A
Booking

AI receptionist

Also called: AI booking assistant, AI phone assistant, chatbot for bookings

An AI receptionist is an AI assistant that answers customer calls and chat messages for a business and helps them book, so enquiries are not missed when staff are busy or the shop is closed.

AI receptionist in Zennly →
Booking

Appointment scheduling software

Also called: appointment booking software, scheduling software, booking app

Appointment scheduling software lets a business take, manage and confirm bookings online instead of by phone or paper. For service businesses such as spas, salons and clinics it also assigns the staff member and room for each booking and prevents overlaps.

Spa and salon booking software →
Security

Audit trail

Also called: audit log, activity log

An audit trail is a permanent record of who did what and when, such as who requested and approved a discount. It lets owners review decisions after the fact without relying on memory.

Discount approval with an audit trail →
B
Salons

Beauty parlour

Also called: parlor, beauty salon, ladies parlour

A beauty parlour is the common name in Nepal and South Asia for a salon offering hair, skin, nails, threading, waxing and bridal makeup services.

Salon booking system in Nepal →
Salons

Beauty parlour software

Also called: parlour software, parlour management system, salon software Nepal

Beauty parlour software is booking and management software for beauty parlours, the common Nepali name for a salon. It takes appointments online, schedules beauticians, tracks packages and discounts, and records payments.

Beauty parlour software in Nepal →
Operations

Booking lifecycle

Also called: appointment status workflow, booking status

The booking lifecycle is the set of stages a booking moves through, such as Pending, Confirmed, In-Progress and Completed. Tracking the stage of every booking shows the owner what is happening across all branches in real time.

Real-time operations dashboard →
Booking

Booking software

Also called: online booking software, reservation software

Booking software lets a business take appointments online and manage them in one calendar. Spas, salons, gyms and clinics use it to replace phone calls and paper books, assign staff and rooms, and track payments per booking.

How to choose booking software in Nepal →
C
Booking

Clinic appointment software

Also called: physiotherapy appointment software, clinic scheduling software

Clinic appointment software schedules practitioners and rooms together, keeps repeat treatments with the same practitioner and records payments per visit. It is not a medical records system.

Clinic appointment software in Nepal →
D
Finance

Daily reconciliation

Also called: end-of-day reconciliation, daily cash-up, till reconciliation, daily close

Daily reconciliation is the end-of-day check that recorded payments match the money actually received, by method (cash, card, online). When each payment is recorded against its booking at the point of service, the daily close is a read rather than a rebuild.

Payment tracking and daily reconciliation →
Finance

Deposit

Also called: booking deposit, advance payment, prepayment

A deposit is a part-payment taken when a booking is made, to hold the slot. It is recorded against the booking, and the balance is recorded when the service is complete. Deposits are common in tattoo studios and for long treatments.

Tattoo studio booking and deposits →
Finance

Discount approval workflow

Also called: discount authorization, discount authorisation, manager approval for discounts

A discount approval workflow requires staff to request a discount and a manager to approve it before it applies. Every request and decision is logged, which prevents untracked discounts and revenue leakage.

Discount management →
Booking

Double booking

Also called: overbooking, scheduling conflict

A double booking happens when the same staff member, room or time slot is booked for two customers at once. Zennly refuses conflicting bookings at database level, so a double booking cannot be saved, even across branches.

Conflict-free booking →
G
Finance

Gift voucher

Also called: prepaid voucher, gift card

A gift voucher is a prepaid value with a unique code and an expiry date that a customer redeems as payment, in one visit or over several. It differs from a discount code because it holds money.

Vouchers in Zennly →
Fitness

Gym management software

Also called: gym software, fitness studio software, gym membership software

Gym management software tracks memberships, renewals, classes and trainers for a gym or fitness studio. It shows who has paid, who has lapsed and how many sessions remain.

Gym membership software in Nepal →
M
Finance

Membership management software

Also called: prepaid membership software, wallet balance software

Membership management software tracks prepaid tiers, wallet balances, validity periods and renewals. Every deposit and deduction is recorded so the balance is always correct.

Memberships in Zennly →
Booking

Multi-branch booking software

Also called: multi-location booking software, multi-site booking software, multi-outlet booking software

Multi-branch booking software manages bookings, staff, rooms and revenue for a business with more than one location in a single system. Owners see every branch at once, and staff can be scheduled to transfer between branches.

Multi-location booking software guide →
Operations

Multi-branch spa management

Also called: multi-location spa software, spa chain software

Multi-branch spa management means running several spa locations from one system, with shared staff, one set of rules for discounts and one daily report across branches.

Multi-branch checklist for Nepal →
N
Booking

No-show

Also called: missed appointment, did not attend (DNA)

A no-show is a client who books an appointment and does not turn up or cancel. For service businesses it means an empty slot that cannot be resold, which is why many studios and clinics take a deposit or send confirmations.

Booking software for spas and salons →
O
Booking

Online booking system

Also called: online appointment booking, web booking, self-booking

An online booking system lets customers choose a service, staff member and time on a web page or phone, and holds the slot straight away. For businesses it replaces phone and chat bookings with one calendar that other staff and branches can see.

Booking software →
P
Finance

Part-payment

Also called: partial payment, deposit and balance, split payment

A part-payment is a partial payment toward a booking, such as a deposit paid up front with the balance settled later. Tracking each part-payment against the booking keeps the settlement status accurate.

Payment tracking →
R
Booking

Resource scheduling

Also called: staff and room scheduling, resource booking

Resource scheduling means booking everything a service needs together, such as the staff member, the room and the time, so that none of them is double-booked. It matters most when a treatment needs both a person and a room.

Staff scheduling and transfers →
Security

Role-based access control

Also called: RBAC, staff permissions, user permissions

Role-based access control limits what each person can see and do according to their job. In Zennly, owners, managers and front-desk staff each get a different level of access to bookings, payments and reports.

Role-based access →
S
Operations

Salon management software

Also called: salon booking software, hair salon software, beauty parlour software

Salon management software handles bookings, stylist schedules, payments and reports for a hair or beauty salon. For salons with several branches it also shows every location in one place and controls discounts.

Salon management software: how to choose →
Finance

Session package

Also called: treatment package, service bundle

A session package is a bundle of sessions for one service sold at a set price, such as ten massages. Each visit redeems one session, and the record shows sessions left and any amount due.

Packages in Zennly →
Operations

Spa management software

Also called: spa booking software, wellness center software, wellness centre software

Spa management software combines online booking, therapist and room scheduling, payments and reporting for a spa or wellness centre. Multi-location spas also need one view of every site, so owners can compare bookings and revenue.

Spa management software: how to choose →
Staff

Staff commission

Also called: therapist commission, commission calculation

Staff commission is extra pay calculated as a percentage of the value of the services a therapist, stylist or trainer completed. It is usually paid on top of a base salary.

How to calculate commission and payroll →
Operations

Staff transfer

Also called: employee transfer between locations, cross-location staffing

A staff transfer schedules an employee to work at a different branch on given days. In a multi-branch system the transfer is planned ahead so the staff member's availability is correct at both locations.

Staff scheduling and transfers →
W
Booking

Walk-in booking

Also called: walk-in appointment, drop-in booking

A walk-in booking is an appointment entered for a customer who arrives without booking in advance. Entering it in the same calendar as online bookings keeps staff and rooms from being promised twice.

Conflict-free booking →
Marketing

Win-back email

Also called: lapsed customer email, re-engagement email

A win-back email is a message sent to a customer who has not visited for a set number of days, inviting them to return. It is a low-cost way to recover lapsed regulars.
