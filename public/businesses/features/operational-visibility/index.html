Things we get asked.

Answers to what owners ask before they switch.

Good managers are valuable. But even the best manager is a bottleneck in an information chain. Real-time visibility doesn't replace your managers – it frees them from the burden of being your information conduit and lets them focus on managing their team.

You don't have to. The value isn't constant monitoring. It's that when something feels off – when you have a hunch, when a manager's summary doesn't add up – you have a source of truth you can go to. That's worth more than any report.

Zennly gives owners a single real-time dashboard showing every branch's bookings, staff status, and payments as they happen — no phone calls or manager check-ins required to know how the day is going.

It is one screen that shows every branch's bookings, staff and payments in real time. Zennly gives spa, salon and gym owners in Nepal that single dashboard, so they stop phoning each manager for updates.
